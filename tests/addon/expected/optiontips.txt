Play outside of raid: Off | Off, sounds only play inside raid instances. On, they play everywhere: the open world, dungeons and battlegrounds (each tab can also turn battlegrounds off).
Show minimap button: Off | The ScubaSounds button on the edge of the minimap. Left-click opens this window, right-click opens the Stats tab, and dragging moves it around the edge. /ss opens this window either way.
Disable Sounds: Off | Mutes every ScubaSounds sound for you, and greys out the sound settings. A sound you would have heard doesn't count as heard (or get announced). The play buttons still preview. /ss off and /ss on do the same.
Replay one-time sounds every time: Off | Can be spammy. Only you hear the replays; nothing goes to guild chat or your group. | More show up here as they're found
Send messages in guild: Off | Lets the addon type in chat for you: announcing sounds the guild discovers, and a few short celebration lines. Off, it never posts in chat. The hidden addon messages that let guildmates hear your sounds still go out, so they hear them either way.
Play far-away guildmates' death sounds: Off | Plays a guildmate's death sound when they die anywhere, not just near you. Deaths you can see play either way.
Play first discovery sounds when found: Off | When a guildmate sets off a sound nobody in the guild has heard yet, it plays for you too, wherever you are, so everyone hears the discovery as it happens.
Play every sound: Off | Very spammy, mostly for debugging. | Plays every sound any guildmate's client plays, skipping cooldowns. Sounds and tabs you've turned off stay off. "See all sounds in chat" shows who played them.
See all sounds in chat: Off | Very spammy, mostly for debugging. | Every sound any guildmate's client plays shows in your chat with who played it and the sound's link (only you see it). Sounds and tabs you've turned off are left out. Works with sounds disabled too.
Show sample raids: Off | Fills the Raids tab with made-up raids using the guild's names, to try it out before any real raids. They're only on your screen and never synced. Untick to see your guild's real raids, which are recorded either way. It turns itself off once, when your guild's first raid is recorded.
Say "tartattack" in guild chat: Off | The first press of each new /startattack macro says it in guild chat. Off, those presses stay quiet.
